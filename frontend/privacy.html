Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What we collect

Analytics. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand anonymous usage — which pages are visited and where visitors come from. This data is aggregated and never sold.

Checkout. When you buy, our payment partners (Dodo Payments and Stripe) process your payment. They collect your email and payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only the order confirmation and your email so we can issue your license key.

License keys. We store issued license keys and anonymised checkout attempts so we can unlock your tools and prevent fraud.

Contact. If you email us at contact@mindnspace.com, we keep your message so we can reply.

What we never collect

Your CV data stays in your browser. The CV and resume builders run entirely on your device — your name, phone number, employment history and photo are never sent to our servers.

Cookies

We use a cookie to remember your license unlock, and Google Analytics sets cookies to measure traffic. No advertising cookies are used.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@mindnspace.com. We reply to every request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.

General information only — not legal advice.
