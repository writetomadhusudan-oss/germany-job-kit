Reference — salary rules since 22 July 2025

  • Standard route: £41,700/year or the occupation's going rate — whichever is higher.
  • Discounted floor: £33,400/year (non-STEM PhD: £37,500) — only with a valid discount.
  • Hourly floor: £17.13/hour, on guaranteed basic gross pay.
  • Skill level: job must be RQF 6 (degree level) or above.
  • English: B1 required; some 2026 sources report B2 from 8 January 2026 — verify on gov.uk.
  • Your sponsor must be a licensed Home Office sponsor; the role must be genuine.

IT SOC codes (2134 programmers/software developers, 2133 IT business analysts, 2135 cybersecurity) are not on the Immigration Salary List — IT applicants normally use the standard £41,700/going-rate test or the new-entrant discount. Source: gov.uk, September 2026. Thresholds change — re-verify before applying.
