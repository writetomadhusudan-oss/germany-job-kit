EU Blue Card Checker

The Blue Card is Germany's fastest work-permit route for skilled professionals. Check your offer against the 2026 thresholds.

Worth knowing

  • No German required for the Blue Card. English is widely used in German IT workplaces.
  • Permanent residence after 21 months with German B1, or 27 months with A1.
  • Family reunification possible from day one; no German required for spouses.
  • Thresholds are updated every year — always confirm the current year's figures before signing.
Not legal advice. General information based on 2026 rules — re-verify at make-it-in-germany.com or the BAMF before applying.
