How the points test works (reference only)

  • The minimum to lodge an Expression of Interest (EOI) is 65 points — but invitation rounds are competitive.
  • ICT and engineering occupations typically need ~85–95+ points to be invited (4 June 2026 invitation round); invitations went to the highest scorers first.
  • 189 is the independent visa (no sponsor needed). 190 adds 5 points with state nomination. 491 adds 15 points but is regional and provisional.
  • Skills assessment from the relevant authority (e.g. ACS for ICT) and a valid English test (≤3 years old) are mandatory before you can be invited.
  • Note: a 2026 points-test reform has been proposed but not enacted as of September 2026 — the table above is the current test.
