🛂 Passeport Talent Checker

France's main skilled-worker routes, checked against the official 2026 thresholds. General information only — not legal advice. Verify at service-public.gouv.fr (fiche F16922).

Official 2026 thresholds

  • Passeport Talent « salarié qualifié » — gross salary ≥ €39,582/year, French master-level diploma, contract > 3 months.
  • Passeport Talent « entreprise innovante / salarié en mission » — same €39,582 threshold; innovative employer or intra-group mobility.
  • EU Blue Card (carte bleue européenne) — gross salary ≥ €59,373/year (1.5× the reference salary), diploma of 3+ years higher education or 5 years relevant experience (3 years in the last 7 for shortage roles), contract ≥ 6 months.

Source: service-public.gouv.fr, fiche F16922 (verified June 2026). Thresholds set by arrêté of 29 August 2025 — fixed euro amounts, no longer SMIC multiples.
