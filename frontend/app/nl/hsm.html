🇳🇱 Highly Skilled Migrant & EU Blue Card Checker

Check a Dutch job offer against the 2026 IND salary thresholds (gross per month, excluding the 8% holiday allowance). Thresholds change every January — the amount in force is the one on your application date. General information only — not legal advice; re-verify at ind.nl.

Your situation

2026 IND thresholds (gross/month, excl. 8% holiday allowance)

  • Highly Skilled Migrant, 30 or older — €5,942
  • Highly Skilled Migrant, under 30 — €4,357
  • HSM reduced criterion (orientation-year / recent graduates within 3 years) — €3,122
  • EU Blue Card, standard — €5,942
  • EU Blue Card, reduced (degree within last 3 years) — €4,754

Effective 1 January 2026. Source: ind.nl — required amounts / income requirements.

💰 The 30% ruling (tax advantage)

  • The full 30% rate still applies throughout 2026 — it drops to a flat 27% from 1 January 2027.
  • 2026 minimum taxable wage: €48,013/year (≈ €68,590 gross/year).
  • Under-30s with a non-Dutch master's degree: €36,497 taxable (≈ €52,139 gross).
  • Cap on the tax-free sum: €262,000. Maximum duration: 5 years.
  • Requires living 150+ km from the Dutch border for at least 16 of the prior 24 months — i.e. genuinely recruited from abroad.

Employers apply for the ruling with the Belastingdienst. General information only — not tax advice.

How the two routes compare

  • Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant): the employer must be an IND-recognised sponsor — your employer files the application for you. 2026 application fee: €423. IND target decision time: ~2 weeks.
  • EU Blue Card: needs a contract of at least 6 months and a recognised higher-education degree (or qualifying experience). No recognised sponsor required. After 12 months on a Dutch Blue Card you gain intra-EU mobility (easier move to another EU country).
  • Recent graduates (orientation-year permit holders, degree within 3 years) get the reduced thresholds on both routes.
