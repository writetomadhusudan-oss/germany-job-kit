Questions

Is this immigration legal advice?

No. The kit gives general information based on publicly available 2026 rules. Visa thresholds and requirements change yearly — always re-verify at official sources like make-it-in-germany.com or the BAMF before applying.

Do I need to speak German to get hired?

Not for the EU Blue Card — there is no German requirement, and English is widely used in German IT workplaces. German does speed up permanent residence (21 months with B1 vs 27 with A1) and helps enormously in daily life.
